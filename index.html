About Me

I am a climate science graduate with an MSc in Applied Meteorology and Climate from the University of Reading. My work focuses on analysing large-scale atmospheric and oceanic systems using climate model output to explore how regional climate patterns evolve under different Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs).

My Master’s dissertation examined the impacts of an Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) collapse on hydroclimate systems in Southern Africa using Python-based climate model analysis.

I am passionate about making climate science accessible through communication and creative writing.

I combine Python workflows, geospatial mapping, and climate communication to translate complex environmental data into clear insights.
